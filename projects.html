Multiple Face Recognition Attendance System

International Journal of Research and Analytical Reviews (IJRAR) 📅 March 14, 2024

This research paper presents an automated attendance management system leveraging face recognition technology. The system employs OpenCV with Haar Cascade classifiers for face detection and the LBPH algorithm for recognition, integrated with an SQL database for attendance logging. The paper demonstrates the system's accuracy, real-time performance, and practical utility in educational and corporate environments.
